## V1《挑灯》（lyleCode 创作）

### 风格提示词（英文，提交原文）

> Chinese dark cinematic art-rock (literary rock); male mid-range lead vocal (warm baritone) singing in Mandarin — low and restrained in the verses, raw and aching in the chorus; a sleepless night meditation, not a love song. Signature hook: a hypnotic, memorable two-bar descending riff played by cello and piano in unison with accordion shadowing — like a slow fall, or a river going down; it opens the song, returns between sections, and haunts the outro. Instrumentation: cello, accordion, piano, bass, live drums (slow, heavy, tom-driven 4/4 with a heartbeat pulse), sparse acoustic guitar. About 96 BPM, 4/4, D minor, dark and spacious, building from near-silence to a cathartic full-band chorus. Verses sparse and intimate, pre-chorus rising, choruses huge and aching; the bridge strips down to cello, accordion and voice (the sword moment), then the band crashes back for the final chorus; outro fades with the descending riff and soft low group vocals.
